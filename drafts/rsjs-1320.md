Following up on WebDAV, I tested Nextcloud and found a route that needs no change to remoteStorage.js.

**A small Nextcloud app makes Nextcloud a remoteStorage server.** [jonocodes/nextcloud-remotestorage](https://github.com/jonocodes/nextcloud-remotestorage) (about 1,700 lines of PHP, public APIs only, no core patches) adds WebFinger, an OAuth consent page with scoped tokens, CORS, and the protocol details Nextcloud's WebDAV is missing. Files stay ordinary Nextcloud files under `remoteStorage/`, so versions, trash and quotas still apply.

On Nextcloud 34 and 35:

- Unmodified rs.js 2.0.0-beta.9 connects from `user@host`, syncs, and syncs a second device without conflicts.
- [api-test-suite](https://github.com/remotestorage/api-test-suite) passes 52/53. The one failure is a false positive caused by Nextcloud's session cookies.
- Apache works as shipped. nginx needs two small additions to the documented config.

Everything is reproducible with [the harness](https://github.com/jonocodes/remotestorage-nextcloud-harness):

- [REPORT-app.md](https://github.com/jonocodes/remotestorage-nextcloud-harness/blob/main/REPORT-app.md) covers the app.
- [REPORT.md](https://github.com/jonocodes/remotestorage-nextcloud-harness/blob/main/REPORT.md) covers a plain-WebDAV backend for rs.js. It works with the WebAppPassword app, but its tokens expire daily.

One finding for rs.js: webfinger.js fetches with `redirect: "manual"`, so in browsers any WebFinger redirect breaks discovery. Nextcloud's nginx config uses one. I'm happy to open a separate issue or PR.

The app isn't in the Nextcloud app store yet, and I haven't tried it on a production install. Feedback is welcome, especially on the scope and token handling.
